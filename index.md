*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

VOW is a personal health-accountability app with an AI coach. It is **in private development and is currently used only by its developer**. It is not available on the App Store and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data VOW handles and how. It will be updated before VOW is ever offered to anyone else.

## Who is responsible

VOW is developed and operated by an individual developer ("we"). Contact: **vowadmin83@gmail.com**.

## What VOW is, and isn't

VOW helps a user track their own health habits and keep the commitments they set themselves. It offers **wellness analysis, not medical advice**. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. It does not recommend starting or stopping medication, and it tells you to speak to a doctor about anything out of range.

## Data VOW handles

Only data the user chooses to connect or enter:

- **Account:** sign-in identifier (Sign in with Apple), display name, settings and preferences.
- **Apple Health:** activity, sleep, heart rate and HRV, body measurements and nutrition, read with the user's permission.
- **Garmin Connect:** sleep, recovery, stress, Body Battery, daily summaries and activities, via the user's own Garmin personal API key.
- **Calendars:** events from the calendar(s) the user selects as their plan (e.g. Outlook). Other calendars aren't stored.
- **WhatsApp:** messages, photos and voice notes the user sends to VOW's coach number, and the coach's replies.
- **Voice input:** voice notes on WhatsApp and speech in the app. Audio is **deleted immediately after transcription** by default. In the app, words are first recognised on the device. The recording is then transcribed by our speech-to-text provider for accuracy, unless the user turns on "Keep my voice on this iPhone".
- **Things the user enters:** workouts, water, recovery sessions, injuries and rehab, goals, notes, and blood-test results the user uploads and confirms.

## How data is used

- To show the user their own progress and to power their personal AI coach.
- **Never** sold, **never** used for advertising, and **never** used to train AI models.
- Apple Health data is used only to provide VOW's health features to the user. It isn't shared with third parties for advertising or data-broking, and it isn't stored in iCloud.

## AI processing

VOW's coach uses Anthropic's Claude API to analyse the user's data and write replies. Only the data needed for a reply is sent, using the user's first name only, with no contact details or ID numbers. Anthropic processes it to generate the response and doesn't use it to train its models under its commercial terms. Data from Garmin is labelled as such wherever it's shown.

## Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored in a private database hosted by **Supabase** in **Singapore**, encrypted in transit and at rest. Each user's rows are isolated by row-level security. Service providers that process data on VOW's behalf:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, file storage, sign-in, server functions |
| Anthropic | AI coach (Claude API) |
| Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) | Sending and receiving WhatsApp messages |
| Microsoft | Reading the selected Outlook calendar |
| Garmin | Source of Garmin Connect data the user connects |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple, Apple Health, push notifications |
| OpenAI | Transcribing voice notes and in-app speech; audio is deleted after transcription |

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the user's account is active. The user can export their data or **permanently delete their account and all associated data**, including uploaded files, at any time, and can disconnect any integration, which deletes its stored credentials. WhatsApp voice notes are deleted after transcription. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact above.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing, by contacting us. If you're in the UK, EU or Hong Kong you also have rights under UK/EU GDPR or the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

## Children

VOW is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Changes

We'll update this page, and its date, when anything changes. Material changes will be notified in the app before they take effect.
